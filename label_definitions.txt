# Your label definitions, one per line, as `label: definition`.
# Copied from the Label Taxonomy section of README.md.

opinion: The poster states a mood, joke or view, bullish or bearish, with no checkable support behind it.
analysis: The poster argues a view or a trade using support a reader could check, such as a number, a catalyst or a causal mechanism.
result: The poster states what they did, hold or got: an outcome (a gain, loss or "I'm up/down") or a bare position with no reason attached.
news: The post relays facts or data about the market or the world, such as an event, a filing or compiled performance numbers, and not the poster's own outcome.
